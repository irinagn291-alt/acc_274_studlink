Thank you for the review. We retested the build under review on a clean device. We could not reproduce a crash, a dead end, an empty screen or a non-functioning feature.

Three behaviours are deliberate. Each can look broken on a short pass.

1. Prove chain stays disabled until two links sit on the chain. One pick, or none, is not enough. Open Fixtures, stay on Card, add two fixtures, tap Home, Draw, or Away on each, then the button works.

2. Tapping Home, Draw, or Away on a fixture closes Fixtures and returns you to Chain. The pick is under Links on this chain. That close is intended. Tap Add the next pick or Fixtures to add another link.

3. House and Season start empty on a clean install. Proof House shows No proved chains, and Season shows No season yet. Both offer Back to the chain. Lists appear after Prove chain, and Season totals fill after Save results on Settle.

Full review path. No account, no login:
- Cold launch may show Loading Studlink, then One chain for the weekend, Shackle, then prove, and One swage, then settle. Tap Continue on the first two panes, then Start proving, or tap Skip.
- Home is marked STUDLINK. The words across the top are Chain, Fixtures, House, Season, and Settings.
- If you see No chain yet, tap Add a fixture card. Stay on Card. Under NEW FIXTURE set Kickoff, type Home side and Away side, and tap Add fixture. Add at least two fixtures. On each row tap Home, Draw, or Away.
- The board then shows This week's payout, Links on this chain, and Add the next pick. With two or more links, tap Prove chain. To change one unstarted pick, tap a row under Links on this chain to open Swage, then choose Home, Draw, or Away. One change is allowed, and only before kickoff.
- After the matches, open Fixtures, choose Settle, mark the real Home, Draw, or Away, and tap Save results. If the chain is not locked, that button still reads Prove chain. Settle with no fixtures shows Nothing to settle.
- House opens Proof House and lists WHOLE or PARTED chains after a proof. Season shows POINTS, PICK HIT RATE, FIXTURE HIT RATE, and WHOLE RATE BY CHAIN LENGTH. Settings holds Replay onboarding, Reset all data, and Contact Studlink. It also states Studlink is a points league only. There is no stake, no balance, no odds and no cash out.
- Share sits on Fixtures. Scan a friend's card, then Continue, is the only path that asks for the camera. CARD CODE appears after at least one fixture.

A clean install does not plant a week. Cards, chains, picks, and results stay on this phone, so an unfinished card or unproved chain is still there when you return. Reset all data, after you confirm, clears the book.
