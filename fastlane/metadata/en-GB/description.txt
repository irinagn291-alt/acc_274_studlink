Studlink is a points-only weekend football league for friends. You link this week's picks into one chain and prove it. The chain scores only if every link holds.

• Home is one chain. This week's payout is the link count squared. Three links score 9. Six score 36.
• Add a fixture card, then type Kick-off, Home side and Away side.
• Tap Home, Draw or Away to add a pick. Add the next pick from the open shackle.
• Prove chain locks the chain when it has at least two links.
• One swage: change one unstarted pick before Proof. A second swage is refused.
• After the matches, open Fixtures, choose Settle, mark the real results and tap Save results. The first miss parts the chain and voids the rest.
• Share a Card code so another phone can scan the same week of fixtures.
• House opens Proof House, which lists WHOLE or PARTED chains and names the snap.
• Season totals POINTS, the longest whole chain, PICK HIT RATE, FIXTURE HIT RATE and WHOLE RATE BY CHAIN LENGTH.
• Settings includes Replay onboarding, Reset all data and Contact Studlink.

Studlink is a points league only. There is no stake, no balance, no odds and no cash out.
